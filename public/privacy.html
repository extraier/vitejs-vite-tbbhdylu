Privacy Policy — FoodSwipe HK

Last updated: September 2, 2026

FoodSwipe HK ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a Hong Kong restaurant and dish discovery application operated by an individual developer (Roger Lau). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (for email/password sign-in)
  • Display name and profile photo URL (provided by your sign-in method)
  • User identifier (a unique Firebase UID)

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. You can sign in with: email + password, Google, or Facebook. The credentials you use to authenticate are processed by the respective provider and are not stored on our servers. We only receive your UID, display name, and profile photo URL.

1.2 Content you create

  • Reviews and ratings (1–5 stars, optional text up to 500 characters, optional photos up to 4)
  • Replies to reviews (text up to 300 characters)
  • Swipe actions (which dishes you liked or passed on)
  • Reported errors for dishes (restaurant name, dish name, reason, optional notes)

1.3 Device and usage data

  • Push notification tokens — if you grant notification permission, we store an FCM token to deliver match alerts and other updates. You can revoke this in the App's settings or your device settings.
  • Crash and error logs — Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymized crash reports and device metadata (OS version, device model, app version) to help us debug issues. No personal content is included.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Display your profile and review history in the App
  • Show your reviews and ratings to other users
  • Notify you when a swipe partner likes the same dish as you
  • Show aggregate ratings (e.g. "4.3 stars from 12 reviews") on dish cards
  • Debug crashes and improve the App
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Show you targeted advertising based on your profile
  • Track your activity across other apps or websites

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase:

  • Cloud Firestore (Singapore region) — your profile, reviews, likes, notifications
  • Firebase Storage (Singapore region) — your review photos
  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in credentials
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification tokens
  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymized crash reports

Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Google may process data outside of Hong Kong. See Firebase Privacy.

4. Public Information

The following is publicly visible to any user (including anonymous users) of the App:

  • Your display name and profile photo on reviews and replies you post
  • Star ratings, comment text, and review photos you submit
  • Your replies to other users' reviews

Other public information is anonymized: e.g. the swipe deck shows dish averages like "4.3 ★ (12 reviews)" but does not reveal which user submitted which rating.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We share data only with:

  • Firebase / Google LLC — as described above
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose that sign-in method (Google receives your auth request; we receive your UID + profile)
  • Facebook Login — if you choose that sign-in method (Facebook receives your auth request; we receive your UID + profile)

We do not share your data with advertisers, analytics companies, or data brokers.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Access, edit, delete

  • Reviews — edit or delete your own reviews from inside the App (dish detail → your review → "刪除" / "更新評價")
  • Replies — delete your own replies from the dish detail screen
  • Account deletion — sign in, then email privacy@foodswipehk.com to request full deletion of your account and all associated data (profile, reviews, replies, likes, notifications). We will delete within 30 days.

Notifications

You can disable push notifications at any time in the App's settings or in your device's notification settings. Disabling removes your FCM token from our database.

Sign-in revocation

You can revoke Google or Facebook sign-in from your Google/Facebook account settings. You can reset your password from the App's sign-in screen.

7. Data Retention

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account or after 24 months of inactivity
  • Reviews you delete are removed immediately; cached copies on Google Cloud may persist for up to 90 days as part of normal backup rotation
  • Crash logs are retained for 90 days

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us to delete it.

9. Security

We use Firebase's security features including encrypted data in transit (HTTPS/TLS), Firestore Security Rules that restrict who can read and write which documents, and Firebase Authentication for sign-in.

No method of transmission over the Internet, however, is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Firebase's default data storage region for this project is asia-southeast1 (Singapore). Google may process data in other regions as part of normal operations. By using the App, you understand your data may be transferred to and processed in those regions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will be announced via an in-app notification on the next time you open the App.
